How to play

  • Four tigers start in the corners. The goats have 20 pieces and move first.
  • Goats are placed one per turn on any empty point. After all 20 are placed, goats slide along a line to an adjacent empty point.
  • Tigers slide to an adjacent empty point, or jump over a goat in a straight line onto an empty point, capturing it.
  • Diagonal lines exist only through the points shown on the board.
  • Tigers win by capturing 5 goats. Goats win by trapping every tiger so none can move.
