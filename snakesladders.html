साँप सिँढी

Climb the ladders, dodge the snakes, reach HOME first

Players

How to play

  • Take turns rolling the dice and moving your pawn forward from START (square 1) towards HOME (square 100).
  • Land at the bottom of a ladder and you climb up to its top.
  • Land on a snake’s head and you slide down to its tail.
  • Roll a 6 and you get another turn.
  • You must roll the exact number to land on 100. If your roll is too high, you stay where you are.
  • First pawn to reach HOME wins.
