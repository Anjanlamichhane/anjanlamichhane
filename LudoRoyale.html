How to play

🎲 Roll a 6 to bring a token out of the yard.
⭐ Tokens on star or start squares can’t be captured.
💥 Land on an opponent to send them home — and roll again.
🔁 A 6 also gives you another turn.
🏁 Get all 4 tokens home with an exact roll to win.
